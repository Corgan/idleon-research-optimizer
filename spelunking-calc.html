Trial settings

Every legal combination in the selected scope receives screening trials. Cave and Normal/Manic mode stay fixed. Belts fill every possible slot from the checked elixirs, including unlocked duplicates. Empty slots remain only when that pool cannot fill them. Memories are optional; none checked means no memories.
