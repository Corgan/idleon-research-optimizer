Permanent rewards

Next-run reward charge

Charge grows with real waiting time, not Paying Respect training. Starting a normal story consumes it. After the linear window it keeps growing, more slowly. Run performance determines the levels earned; there is no fixed levels/day conversion.

Paying Respect training

Training hours strengthen minigame stats and unlock milestones. They are not permanent reward levels and do not speed up reward charge. The daily figure is one character's single 24-hour claim; claims must exceed 3,599 seconds and round down.

Bravery

Right-most sword damage boost

Run state is not saved. Enter the current enemy and boost count. For the finishing-blow calculation, let every other sword land before the right-most sword; an earlier kill ends the fight before a later sword can grant a boost.

Fresh-roll odds use continuous uniform damage and no rethrows. A rethrow replaces the last sword's old damage; it does not add another hit. Its new roll uses the current rethrow count before that count decreases. The HP input should exclude that sword's old hit when planning a rethrow.

Justice

Response advisor

A local worksheet, not a live-game connection. Use current values after this visitor's arrival effects. Select the case you actually received; the advisor does not predict random visitors. Nothing here changes your save.

Active deals, promises & investments

Unchecked effects are absent. A checked numeric effect may legitimately contain zero.

Record the response and, for random offers, the outcome that actually occurred. Then select the next observed case and open it once to settle timers and visitor payments. To join an ongoing story, start a fresh worksheet, then enter its case number, current stats and active effects directly. The rare-glass offer uses the source's 1-in-3 roll, despite its 1-in-4 description.

Wisdom
