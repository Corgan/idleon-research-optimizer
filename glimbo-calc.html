Price formula and breakpoint

Before companion discounts, the price is (1 + n + 1.5 × n) × scalen, with a 25% Event Shop discount if owned. Below one billion, the companion multiplier has a 0.2 minimum and the price is floored, with a minimum of one. At one billion or above, the minimum multiplier becomes 0.01 and no rounding is applied. Pet+ can therefore cause a downward jump. Here n is this material's completed paid trades, not its Vault upgrade level. Discounts are held fixed throughout each curve.
